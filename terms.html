2. Personal tracking, not medical care

OvaLens is not a diagnostic or contraception tool. It cannot confirm ovulation, fertility, pregnancy or a medical condition. Do not use its calendar, test labels, charts or AI output to prevent pregnancy, choose treatment or make medical decisions. Follow your test manufacturer’s instructions and consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Calendar dates are rough estimates derived from the cycle settings and records available. An LH test result, a temperature pattern or a calendar marker does not establish that ovulation occurred. Irregular cycles, missing records and other factors can make estimates inaccurate. “Low”, “High”, “Peak” and similar saved labels describe recorded test assessments, not measured hormone concentrations or a diagnosis. A manually entered T/C ratio is your supplied value.

Negative-photo adjustments are a visual aid. They do not create a laboratory measurement or make an unreadable test reliable. This app and its support address are not emergency services.

AI requires a connection, a supported provider region and your explicit data-sharing permission. It may be unavailable or limited by quotas, provider changes or technical failures. Manual journal features remain available without optional AI. See the Privacy Policy before sharing photos or reports.
